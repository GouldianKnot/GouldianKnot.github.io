The Birds of Australia — Volume I

John Gould, 1840–48 — the full, much larger successor to the 1837–38 Synopsis. Volume I covers the Raptores: eagles, ospreys, falcons, hawks, and owls, 36 species in all, each with its own hand-coloured plate. First volume of a planned 7-volume build, one volume at a time.
