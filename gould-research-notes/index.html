Gould’s Most-Cited Authority Was a Minister Who Held the Last British Great Auk

John Fleming was cited by Gould 44 times across Birds of Great Britain — more than almost anyone else in the Library. He was also a Free Church minister who spent his career trying to reconcile geology with Genesis, and in 1821 he was handed, alive, the last Great Auk Britain would ever see.

What the Plate Doesn’t Show: Lorenz and the Birds Gould Only Drew

Gould never read Konrad Lorenz — Lorenz was born the year after Gould died. But two of Lorenz’s most famous research subjects, the Jackdaw and the Grey Lag Goose, are birds this gallery already has on the wall. A look at what a static 1870s plate could show, and what a goose that had imprinted on a man in a hat could not have told him.
